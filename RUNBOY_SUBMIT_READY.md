## 4. App Information Text
### Subtitle
Endless Runner Action Game

### Description
RunBoy is a fast-paced endless runner game that tests your reflexes and quick decision-making skills.

Navigate through three lanes while avoiding obstacles and collecting coins. How high can you score?

How to Play:
- Swipe LEFT or RIGHT to change lanes and navigate around obstacles
- Swipe UP to jump over barriers
- Swipe DOWN to slide under obstacles
- Collect yellow coins for bonus points
- Avoid red, orange, and purple obstacles
- Pause anytime with the pause button
- Compete for the highest score

Features:
- Endless runner gameplay with progressive difficulty
- 3-lane navigation system for strategic movement
- Jump and slide mechanics for obstacle avoidance
- Coin collection system with bonus points
- Dynamic difficulty that increases with score
- High score tracking and persistence
- Simple, intuitive touch controls
- Smooth performance on iPhone
- No ads or in-app purchases
- Works offline

Download RunBoy now and start your endless run!
